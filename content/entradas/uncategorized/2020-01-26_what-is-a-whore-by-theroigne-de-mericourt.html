What is a whore?
A girl who, having cleared herself of all modesty, does not blush when she surrenders herself to the sensual and carnal pleasures of men
What qualities should a whore have? Three essential qualities:Are these those qualities? Cheekiness, complacency and metamorphosis
What do we understand by impudence? We understand that a girl who devotes herself to this libinious office should not be ashamed of anything. All parts of her body must be for men as they are for herself. That is, he must show his breasts his pubis and his ass to the stranger whom he entertains with the same indifference with which to teach an honest woman’s hand, that is, without the slightest blush.
What do we understand by metamorphosis?
We understand that a true whore, hidden behind the resources of her art, must be like Proteo, know how to adopt any form, vary the positions of pleasure depending on time, circumstances and the nature of temperaments. An ill-known and agitated whore must modify a particular study of the different variations that seek to please men, as there are differences between entertaining a cold man, or a blond or a dark and brown man; between exciting a youngster or a sensual old man. The most imperative nature in some and more moderate in others, consequently demands different titillations, more voluptuous syllables, more libertine stimulants.
